## 🌸 About me

I'm the comms lead for **Second Nature**, the hometel brand from Lamington Group (formerly room2). I look after brand, content and social, which mostly comes down to being responsible for how the whole thing sounds.

- 💼 Seven-ish years across **hospitality, FMCG and agency**
- 🖼️ A **History of Art** degree underneath it all, which I use more than you'd think
- ✨ At my best turning a fuzzy brief into something with an actual point of view
- 🎨 Reasonably dangerous in **Figma**
- 📍 Based in **London**

## 🍓 Off the clock

I'm building **food no thought**, a deliberately un-precious food and writing project (still a work in progress 🫶). I've collected records since I was thirteen and I think about food a worrying amount, so it was only a matter of time.
